Scoped access for MCP agents.

Your agent connects to MCP servers — and everything it does stays inside the permissions you granted.

  • Every action is scoped — nothing runs unchecked.
  • A hijacked or confused agent can't reach past what you allowed.
  • Sub-agents get less access, never more.

So when something goes wrong — a bad instruction, a compromised tool, a prompt injection — the damage is already bounded.

Works with Claude Code, Codex, and any MCP agent, via a PreToolUse hook.
